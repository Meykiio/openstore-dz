# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this community:

- Being patient with people who are not developers. Most people installing this are shop
  owners, not frontend engineers. "This is confusing" is useful feedback, not an insult.
- Being direct about technical disagreement without being personal.
- Assuming good faith, especially across language. If your French is weak and your
  Arabic is strong, or the reverse, that is not a reason to dismiss someone's point.
- Accepting that a non-technical shop owner's report can outrank a developer's
  architectural opinion about what the product should be.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or insults, public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Dismissing someone's contribution because of their nationality or how they write.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer through a private GitHub message or at
`https://github.com/Meykiio/openstore-dz/security/advisories/new`.

Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow this code of conduct
may be removed from the project.

## Scope

This applies in project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and
anywhere someone is representing the project publicly.
